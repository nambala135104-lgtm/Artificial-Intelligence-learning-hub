What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to the simulation of human intelligence in machines designed to think, reason, learn, and solve problems like humans.

Real-World Examples

  • Virtual Assistants (Siri, Alexa)
  • Autonomous Vehicles (Tesla)
  • Chess & Go engines (AlphaZero)

What is Machine Learning?

Machine Learning (ML) is a subfield of AI that enables systems to learn and improve performance automatically from experience and data without explicit programming.

Real-World Examples

  • Spam Filtering in Email
  • Recommendation Engines (Netflix, Spotify)
  • Predictive Financial Trading

Where is AI Used?

AI applications are transformed across diverse industries, automating complex processes and driving automated decision-making.

Domains & Applications

  • Healthcare: Medical Image Diagnostics
  • Finance: Fraud Detection Systems
  • Engineering: Predictive Maintenance
